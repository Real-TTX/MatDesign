MatDesign

How the Mat apps are meant to look. The rulebook lists the rules, the components show them at work, and MatUi is the CSS and script an app copies to follow them.

130 rules in 14 areas, 24 apps, 91 design tokens and 112 icons.

Rulebook Get MatUi

The rules you notice first

The menu is on the left with the logo above it, the breadcrumb sits above the content, every list has a toolbar, and the buttons are under the content. The shell shows it.

  • SHELL-01 The menu is on the left, the logo above it The menu is always on the left.
  • SHELL-03 The foot of the menu At the bottom of the menu, in this order from top to bottom: the 3-state theme button together with the user, and below them the version.
  • SHELL-07 The menu scrolls on its own The menu and the content scroll separately.
  • SHELL-08 A breadcrumb above every page The content always has a breadcrumb above it: Section / Page / Detail.
  • LIST-03 Search is a button or a field; filter and sort are buttons that open dialogs In the toolbar Filter and Sort are separate buttons.
  • FORM-01 Buttons are always under the content On every create and edit page the buttons are under the content of the form, never in the page head and never floating beside it.
  • DIALOG-03 On a phone every dialog is full screen Below 640 px every dialog fills the screen.
  • IDENT-01 One colour per app Every app has its own accent colour, and it is the only colour an app chooses.
